Privacy Policy for LifePattern AI

Last updated: November 2, 2025

Introduction

LifePattern AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Username: A unique identifier you choose or we generate for you
  • Email Address: For account recovery and communication (if provided)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifier

Health and Fitness Data

  • Activity Data: Steps, exercise, movement patterns
  • Location Data: For context-aware recommendations (with your permission)
  • Health Metrics: Data you choose to share from HealthKit (iOS) or Google Fit (Android)
  • Usage Patterns: How you interact with the app to improve our AI recommendations

Technical Data

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited
  • Crash Reports: Technical information to fix bugs and improve performance
  • Analytics: App usage statistics to understand feature adoption

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  1. Provide Health Insights: Generate personalized AI-powered recommendations
  2. Detect Patterns: Identify changes in your routine (drift detection)
  3. Improve Services: Enhance app functionality and user experience
  4. Communicate: Send important updates and notifications (with permission)
  5. Security: Protect your account and prevent fraud

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive data stored using device-level security (Keychain/SecureStore)
  • Authentication: Token-based authentication with automatic expiration
  • No Plaintext Passwords: We never store your passphrase in plaintext

Data Location

  • Backend Servers: Hosted on Render.com (United States)
  • Database: Secure PostgreSQL database with encrypted connections
  • Backups: Encrypted backups stored securely

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Logs: System logs retained for 90 days for security purposes

Data Sharing

We DO NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your personal data to third parties
  • ❌ Share your health data with advertisers
  • ❌ Use your data for marketing without permission
  • ❌ Share your information with social media platforms

We MAY Share With:

  • ✅ Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Render.com) for app functionality
  • ✅ AI Processing: Our secure AI service for generating insights (no third-party AI services)
  • ✅ Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • ✅ Business Transfer: In case of merger or acquisition (you'll be notified)

  3. Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  4. Export: Download your data in a portable format
  5. Opt-Out: Disable analytics and marketing communications
  6. Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time

If you connect Google Fit:
