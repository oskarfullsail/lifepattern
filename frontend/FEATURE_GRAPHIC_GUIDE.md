# Google Play Store Feature Graphic Guide

## Requirements

**Feature Graphic Specifications:**
- **Dimensions**: Exactly 1024 x 500 pixels
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **File Size**: Up to 15 MB
- **Required**: Yes (mandatory field)

## What is a Feature Graphic?

The feature graphic is a promotional banner that appears at the top of your app's listing in Google Play Store. It's the first visual element users see and should:
- Showcase your app's key features
- Be visually appealing and professional
- Clearly communicate your app's value proposition
- Include your app name/branding
- Be readable on mobile devices

## Design Guidelines

### Best Practices

1. **Keep it Simple**: Don't overcrowd with too much text or elements
2. **Readable Text**: Use large, clear fonts (minimum 24px for body text)
3. **Brand Colors**: Use your app's color scheme (#5BA8DB for LifePattern AI)
4. **High Contrast**: Ensure text is readable against backgrounds
5. **Mobile-First**: Design for small screens (users view on phones)
6. **No UI Elements**: Don't include device frames or UI mockups
7. **Safe Zone**: Keep important content in the center 80% (edges may be cropped)

## Quick Creation Steps (Using Canva)

1. **Sign up/Login** to Canva (free account works)

2. **Create Custom Design**
   - Click "Create a design"
   - Set custom size: 1024 x 500 pixels

3. **Add Background**
   - Use your brand color (#5BA8DB) or gradient
   - Or use a subtle pattern/texture

4. **Add App Name**
   - Large, bold text: "LifePattern AI"
   - Use white or dark text for contrast
   - Font size: 60-80px

5. **Add Tagline**
   - Smaller text below app name
   - Example: "AI-Powered Lifestyle Tracking"
   - Font size: 30-40px

6. **Add Visual Elements**
   - App icon (if you have one)
   - Abstract health/lifestyle graphics
   - Icons representing features (sleep, exercise, etc.)

7. **Export**
   - Click "Download"
   - Choose "PNG" format
   - Ensure it's 1024x500px

## Upload Instructions

1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Select your app: **LifePattern AI**
3. Go to **Store presence** → **Store listing**
4. Scroll to **Graphics** section
5. Find **Feature graphic**
6. Click **Upload** or **Add assets**
7. Select your 1024x500px image
8. Wait for upload to complete
9. Click **Save**
